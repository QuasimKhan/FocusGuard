FocusGuard is an open-source digital wellbeing and study focus tool for Android.
- Block distracting apps or enforce a 15-second mindful pause before opening
- Automatically block YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels while keeping regular videos and DMs open
- Daily Auto-Lock Window and Deep-Work Study Timer with tamper-resistant settings protection
- Local VPN DNS domain blocker with custom rules and comic focus nudges
